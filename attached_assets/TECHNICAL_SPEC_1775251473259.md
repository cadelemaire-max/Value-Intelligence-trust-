# Technical Specifications & Internal Logic

This document details the mathematical and algorithmic foundations of the Football Prediction & Betting Analytics platform.

## 1. Prediction Engine Architecture

The platform uses a **Hybrid Ensemble** approach, combining two distinct models to produce a final probability distribution.

### A. Random Forest Classifier (ML)
- **Library**: `ml-random-forest` (TypeScript).
- **Features**:
  - `home_team_id`, `away_team_id` (Label Encoded).
  - `home_xG`, `away_xG` (Rolling averages from the last 5 matches).
- **Labels**:
  - `2`: Home Win
  - `1`: Draw
  - `0`: Away Win
- **Training**: 50 trees, trained on `data/enriched_historical.csv`.

### B. Poisson Distribution Model (Statistical)
- **Logic**: Calculates the probability of a specific number of goals being scored by each team based on their average attacking and defensive strengths.
- **Formula**: $P(k; \lambda) = \frac{\lambda^k e^{-\lambda}}{k!}$
- **Implementation**:
  - `home_lambda`: (Home Attack Strength * Away Defense Strength * League Average Goals).
  - `away_lambda`: (Away Attack Strength * Home Defense Strength * League Average Goals).

### C. Ensemble Merging
- The final probability is a weighted average of the ML model and the Poisson model.
- **Weighting**: 70% ML, 30% Poisson (adjustable in `server.ts`).

---

## 2. Monte Carlo Simulation

To generate a full scoreline distribution, the platform runs 10,000 simulations per match.

1.  **Input**: `home_lambda` and `away_lambda` from the Poisson model.
2.  **Process**:
    - For each simulation, generate a random number of goals for each team using a Poisson random variable.
    - Record the resulting score (e.g., 2-1, 0-0).
3.  **Output**:
    - **Outcome Probabilities**: % of simulations resulting in Home Win, Draw, or Away Win.
    - **Score Probabilities**: % of simulations resulting in specific scores (e.g., "1-0: 14%").
    - **Over/Under 2.5**: % of simulations with total goals > 2.5.

## 3. Gemini AI Reasoning Layer

Gemini Pro (`gemini-3-flash-preview`) acts as a "Bayesian Refinement" layer.

- **Prompting Strategy**:
  - **System Instruction**: "You are a professional football analyst and Bayesian reasoner."
  - **Context**: Structured match data (xG, form, H2H) + Unstructured context (injuries, weather, league news).
- **Output**:
  - **Refined Probability**: Gemini's adjustment to the base ML probability.
  - **Reasoning**: Natural language explanation.
  - **Risk Warning**: Specific tactical or situational risks.

## 4. Performance Analytics Engine

The platform calculates several metrics to evaluate model "Alpha" (edge over the market).

### A. Brier Score
- Measures the accuracy of probabilistic predictions.
- **Formula**: $BS = \frac{1}{N} \sum_{t=1}^{N} (f_t - o_t)^2$
- where $f_t$ is the predicted probability and $o_t$ is the actual outcome (1 or 0).

### B. AUC-ROC (Area Under the Receiver Operating Characteristic Curve)
- Evaluates the model's ability to distinguish between classes (e.g., Home Win vs. Not Home Win).
- Calculated using the trapezoidal rule over the TPR (True Positive Rate) and FPR (False Positive Rate) at various thresholds.

### C. Kelly Criterion (Stake Optimization)
- Suggests the optimal fraction of the bankroll to wager.
- **Formula**: $f^* = \frac{bp - q}{b}$
- where $b$ is the decimal odds - 1, $p$ is the model's probability, and $q = 1 - p$.

---

## 5. Automated Retraining Pipeline

The system monitors its own performance and triggers a retrain when accuracy drifts.

1.  **Trigger**: `Brier Score > 0.22` or `AUC-ROC < 0.65` over the last 50 matches.
2.  **Process**:
    - Appends the latest settled match results to `data/enriched_historical.csv`.
    - Calls `mlEngine.train()` to update the Random Forest model.
    - Logs the new performance metrics for comparison.
