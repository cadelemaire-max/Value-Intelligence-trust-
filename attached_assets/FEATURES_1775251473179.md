# Application Features & User Guide

This document provides a detailed breakdown of the user-facing features and workflows within the Football Prediction & Betting Analytics platform.

## 🏠 Dashboard (Main View)

The Dashboard is the central hub for discovering value and placing bets.

### 1. Upcoming Fixtures
- **Live Data Fetching**: Fetches the next 10-20 matches from top European leagues (Premier League, La Liga, Bundesliga, etc.).
- **Team Metrics**: Displays recent form, xG (Expected Goals) averages, and H2H (Head-to-Head) history.
- **Market Odds**: Real-time odds for Home Win, Draw, and Away Win.

### 2. Prediction Engine (The "Brain")
- **ML Probability**: Shows the output of the Random Forest model (e.g., "Home Win: 65%").
- **Poisson Score Distribution**: A heatmap or list of the most likely exact scores (e.g., "2-1: 12.5%").
- **Monte Carlo Simulation**: A visual representation of 10,000 simulated outcomes.
- **Edge Detection**: Highlights matches where the model's probability is significantly higher than the market odds (e.g., "Edge: +8.5%").

### 3. Gemini AI Insights
- **Reasoning**: Natural language explanation of *why* the model favors an outcome.
- **Risk Profile**: A "Low", "Medium", or "High" risk rating with specific warnings (e.g., "Key striker injured", "Heavy rain expected").
- **Bayesian Refinement**: Gemini's adjustment to the base ML probability based on contextual factors.

## 💼 Portfolio Management

Track and optimize your betting strategy in real-time.

### 1. Active Bets
- **Live Status**: Real-time updates on match progress (Score, Minute, Status).
- **Cash-Out Suggestions**: AI-driven advice on whether to hold or exit a position early.
- **Exposure Tracking**: Visual breakdown of total stake and potential return.

### 2. Parlay Builder
- **Correlated Outcome Protection**: Prevents adding bets that are logically or statistically linked (e.g., "Team A to Win" and "Team A to Score Over 1.5 Goals" in a parlay).
- **Odds Multiplier**: Automatically calculates the combined odds and potential payout.
- **Kelly Suggestion**: Recommends a lower stake for parlays due to increased variance.

---

## 📊 Analytics & Performance

Deep-dive into the model's historical and real-time accuracy.

### 1. Real-Time Performance
- **ROI (Return on Investment)**: The primary metric for strategy success.
- **Win Rate**: Percentage of successful predictions.
- **Brier Score**: A measure of how close the predicted probabilities were to the actual outcomes.
- **AUC-ROC**: A statistical measure of the model's discriminative power.

### 2. Backtesting Simulator
- **Historical Replay**: Run your current strategy against the last 1,000 matches.
- **Equity Curve**: A chart showing how your bankroll would have evolved over time.
- **Drawdown Analysis**: Identifies the largest peak-to-trough decline in bankroll.

---

## ⚙️ Model Management (Admin)

Controls for maintaining the prediction engine.

### 1. Automated Retraining
- **Thresholds**: Set the Brier Score or AUC-ROC level that triggers a retrain.
- **Data Enrichment**: Automatically appends new match results to the training dataset.
- **Model Versioning**: Tracks performance across different versions of the ML model.

### 2. API Configuration
- **Football-Data.org**: Manage API keys and rate limits.
- **Gemini Pro**: Configure AI reasoning intensity and token usage.

---

## 🛡 Responsible Betting

- **Bankroll Management**: The app enforces a "Max 5% per bet" rule by default.
- **AI Risk Warnings**: Gemini will explicitly warn against "chasing losses" or high-variance bets.
- **Self-Exclusion**: Options to lock the portfolio for a set period.
